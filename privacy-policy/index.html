PRIVACY POLICY

How service-request information is handled

This page explains the current website experience. The online request form does not send or store your information on a Homnaro server. When you choose the WhatsApp handoff, the details you enter are used to prepare a message in your browser.

Information you choose to share

When contacting Homnaro by phone or WhatsApp, you may choose to share your name, contact details, service requirement, location and issue description so your request can be understood.

Changes to this website

If Homnaro later connects a booking form to a backend or other provider, this policy should be updated before that feature is enabled. Please contact Homnaro directly with privacy questions.
